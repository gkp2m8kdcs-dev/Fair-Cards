Frequently Asked Questions

The app shows a disclaimer on first launch. Is this a real-money game?

No. Fair Cards is a single-player entertainment game that does not involve real money, real prizes, in-app purchases, or betting. The disclaimer explains that the doubloons and prizes inside the game are purely virtual and have no cash value. You only need to accept it once on your device.

Where is my gameplay data stored?

Everything — your avatar, stats, achievements, daily-challenge history, and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone. It is never sent to a server, because the game has no server. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Can I reset my progress?

Yes. Go to Settings → Reset progress inside the App. This clears all locally stored data, including statistics, achievements, and your avatar. You can also delete and reinstall the App, which has the same effect.

How do I change my avatar?

Open the Profile tab, tap your avatar, and choose Take photo (camera) or Choose from library. You can also pick one of the built-in illustrated avatars. The photo stays on your device — nothing is uploaded.

The daily challenge — how does it work?

Every day the App generates a single fixed-seed table. Everyone who plays that day gets the same prize layout and the same AI opponents, so results are directly comparable. You can replay the same day as many times as you like to beat your own score.

Sound and haptics aren't playing.
  • Check the in-app toggle: Settings → Sound and Settings → Haptics.
  • Make sure your device is not in Silent mode (the ring/silent switch on the side of the iPhone).
  • Confirm system-wide haptics are enabled in iOS Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics.
The camera or photo library permission was denied. How do I grant it again?

Open iOS Settings → Fair Cards and toggle Camera or Photos to on. Then return to the App. If you don't want to grant permission, you can keep using the default illustrated avatar — the game is fully playable without these permissions.

Does Fair Cards work offline?

Yes. The full game works offline by design. You only need an internet connection the first time you download or update the App from the App Store.

Is there a multiplayer mode?

Not in this version. Fair Cards is single-player only, against two computer-controlled opponents. Adding pass-and-play or online multiplayer is on the roadmap but not currently shipping.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email Sitikhodijah00232@gmail.com with the subject "Fair Cards Feedback". For bugs, please include the steps that led to the problem and — if possible — a screen recording.

How do I request a refund?

Because Fair Cards is free with no in-app purchases, there is nothing to refund. If you believe a charge appears on your statement, please contact Apple Support directly through reportaproblem.apple.com — we cannot access your App Store account or transactions.
