How to play.

Each round the same three moves, then a new twist. Within a few hands the table feels like an old routine.

Open the bidding

Five prizes sit on the table. Two computer rivals and you take turns bidding doubloons from your purse.

Haggle with the merchant

Between rounds the merchant offers a side deal — a swap, a private offer, or a flat lie. Read the room.

Close the fair

Win prizes, score points, and watch your career stats climb. Take a fresh photo for your profile and play another hand.
