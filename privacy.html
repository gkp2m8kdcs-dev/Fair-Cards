Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026 · Last updated: January 1, 2026

Short version. Fair Cards does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not require an account. All gameplay data — your stats, achievements, and avatar — is stored locally on your device. The game works fully offline.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fair Cards (the "App", "we", "us") does and does not collect when you use our single-player card game. We have designed the App so that, by default, nothing about you leaves your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address).
  • Account credentials — there are no accounts.
  • Gameplay telemetry, match results, or device identifiers.
  • Location, contacts, calendars, or microphone data.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data.
  • Crash logs sent to third parties.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your iPhone, in the App's sandboxed storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Player profile: the nickname and avatar image (if you choose one) that you create inside the App.
  • Gameplay state: current doubloon balance, custom card-back preference, sound and haptic settings.
  • Career statistics: wins, losses, longest streak, achievements unlocked, daily-challenge history.
  • Disclaimer acceptance: a flag indicating you have read the in-game disclaimer.

You can erase all locally stored data by deleting the App from your device. Reinstalling starts you from a clean slate.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

3.1 Camera (optional)

If you choose to take a custom avatar photo, iOS will ask for camera permission. The App uses the camera only to capture a still image you select as your avatar. The captured image is stored locally in the App's sandbox and is not uploaded, shared, or transmitted.

3.2 Photo Library — Read (optional)

If you choose to pick an existing photo as your avatar, iOS will ask for photo-library permission. The selected image is copied into the App's sandbox and is not uploaded, shared, or transmitted.

3.3 Photo Library — Write (optional)

Only if you tap "Save result to Photos" after a game, the App will request permission to add an image to your library. The image is generated locally from the final-score screen.

You can deny any of these permissions and still play the full game. The default avatar is a generated illustration that requires no permission at all.

4. No Network Communication

The App does not establish outbound network connections for gameplay, statistics, or matchmaking. Where iOS itself connects to Apple's servers (for example to validate receipts for App Store downloads), that communication is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

5. Children

The App does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because we collect no personal data at all, no special handling is required.

6. Third Parties

The App does not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, social-login SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no third parties that receive your data through the App.

7. International Transfers

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, no international data transfer occurs through the App.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, or Brazil's LGPD), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data we hold about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, these rights are automatically satisfied — but you can still contact us at the address below and we will respond.

To exercise any rights, simply delete the App to erase all locally stored data, or contact us at Sitikhodijah00232@gmail.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain no data on our side. Data on your device is retained until you delete the App or clear App data in iOS Settings.

10. Security

All local storage is protected by iOS sandboxing and (where enabled) the device passcode or Face/Touch ID. The App does not store or transmit any payment card data, because the App is free and contains no in-app purchases.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the App. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot email you about changes; please revisit this page from time to time.
